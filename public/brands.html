The shared fabric

Loom, KnitNet, Fiber and Plexus are not separate products. They are four lenses on one machine.

🧬 Content-addressed

Every stitch, patch and yarn has a CID. Deduplication and verification are built in.

🔄 CRDT merge

Two local patches converge without consensus, locks or a central coordinator.

🔐 DID identity

Every yarn is owned by a decentralised identifier. Accountability without a platform.

🌐 Offline-first

Weave locally, sync via epidemic gossip when a peer appears. Like git, but for knowledge.
